Experience

  1. 2026–present

    Fulbright PhD Researcher

    Royal College of Surgeons in Ireland, Dublin

    Psychiatric neuroscience and epidemiology, on psychedelics and trauma. Measuring inflammatory and neuroplasticity markers of response to DMT-assisted therapy for alcohol use disorder within the Yale randomized controlled trial in US veterans. Leading a scoping review of trauma and biological markers in psychedelic studies.

  2. 2025–present

    Research Assistant

    Adversity, Brain, Novel Therapeutics (ABN) Lab, Yale School of Medicine

    Contributed to three studies: DMT for alcohol use disorder, MEAI for alcohol use disorder, and psychedelic virtual reality. Drafted SOPs, screened participants, administered the MEQ and EDI, processed blood samples, and presented at journal club.

  3. Present

    Co-founder, Executive Director and Head of Research

    The Psyche, Temple University

    A plain-language psychedelic science education group (thepsyche.org).

  4. 2024–present

    Graduate Researcher

    CHEER Laboratory, Temple University

    Analyzing survey data in SAS to find what increases anal cancer prevention among men who have sex with men using PrEP, and building a social media campaign on prevention.

  5. From 2024

    Teaching Assistant and Interim Instructor of Record

    School of Public Health, Temple University

    Took full responsibility for Anatomy and Physiology II during the primary faculty member’s leave: lectures, laboratory sections, assessment and course administration.

  6. 2022–2024

    Analytical Chemist 1

    Eurofins Scientific, Malvern, PA

    HPLC, chromatography, spectroscopy and mass spectrometry assays in a GMP laboratory, including method development and validation.

  7. 2021–2022

    Undergraduate Researcher and Biology Tutor

    Rowan University, Glassboro, NJ

    Neuroscience research on age- and trauma-related disease; supervised five undergraduate research assistants; tutored hundreds of students in biology.

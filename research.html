Ongoing

DMT-assisted therapy for alcohol use disorder: who responds?

RCSI · 2026–present · Fulbright PhD

The question

Why do some people with alcohol use disorder benefit from DMT-assisted therapy more than others?

Why it matters

If inflammatory and neuroplasticity markers predict response, treatment can be matched to the people most likely to benefit.

Where it stands

Analysing plasma inflammatory and neurotrophic markers (IL-6, TNF-α, IL-10, suPAR, BDNF) from the Yale randomized controlled trial of DMT-assisted therapy in US veterans, alongside an ALSPAC birth-cohort analysis.

Manuscript

Anal cancer prevention among MSM using PrEP

Temple University, CHEER Lab · 2024–2026

The question

What do men who have sex with men on PrEP know about anal cancer, and what would get them screened?

Why it matters

Prevention can start before screening age through HPV vaccination and anticipatory guidance. The main barriers were limited information and unclear service availability, not reluctance.

Where it stands

Mixed-methods study: survey of 137 PrEP users and 17 interviews. Poster at Temple Research Day, 2025. Manuscript awaiting review.
